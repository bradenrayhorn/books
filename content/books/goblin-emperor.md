I liked this book.

The world itself was amazing. I had a vivid picture
in my head of all the grand buildings and halls. It felt very expansive and detailed.

The main character was easy to like and root for. I was wishing for more by the end of the book.

At times, trying to keep all of the names straight was difficult.
It was sometimes confusing what was a title and what was a name. Heck, there was about 4 pages of index
dedicated to listing every character mentioned in the book. Tough to keep them straight!
I imagine reading it a second time it would be at least a little easier.
